Real country shapes in [fixed scenes](/data-xplorer/guides/fixed-layouts/): Natural Earth's 110m countries from `world-atlas`, projected once with `d3-geo` into one set of map units, and drawn as `region`s with their own `outline`s. Continents enter to their countries, which land exactly where the continent was drawn. Lesotho is a hole in South Africa's outline, and each label sits at its country's `labelAt`, the middle of its biggest piece.

This one is an app of its own, `apps/world` in the repository, built on the published package. The
frame below runs it, built from exactly these files. Start with `source.ts`; `data.ts` is the
projection, which the explorer never sees. `main.tsx` keeps the focus in the URL hash, builds a
breadcrumb from `onSceneChange`, and shows the selected country from `onNodeSelect`.
