<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Any data" icon="puzzle">
    You describe each scene through a small contract: a heading, groups, items and the links
    between them. The explorer never walks your data, so cycles cost nothing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Continuous" icon="rocket">
    No page loads between levels: the next scene loads while the viewer zooms towards it and
    fades in inside the item they are entering.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accessible" icon="open-book">
    Beside the canvas, an outline of real links and buttons, read aloud and reachable by keyboard.
    Every item has a page of its own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Small and lazy" icon="seti:typescript">
    React 18 or 19 and PixiJS 8. PixiJS loads only when the explorer first mounts. Typed
    throughout, ESM only.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Try it

Teams, then people, then one person's neighbourhood. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan,
double-click to enter.
