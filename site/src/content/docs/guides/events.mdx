Every payload is plain data: your own items, headings, foci and numbers, never a PixiJS object. An
item comes back exactly as your source wrote it, including its `data`, typed by the source's second
type parameter (`createSceneSource<Focus, Data>`).

| Event | Fires | Payload |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `onSettle` | The camera came to rest on a scene, once per burst of motion. | the focus |
| `onSceneChange` | Every scene swap, immediately, including a scene filling in as it streams. Good for a breadcrumb. | `{ heading, focus, scene }` |
| `onNodeSelect` | A confirmed single click or tap on an item, or `null` on empty space. | the item, or `null` |
| `onNodeHover` | The item under the mouse changes. Mouse only. | the item, or `null` |
| `onNodeActivate` | A double-click or double-tap on an item, or Enter on its outline entry. | `{ item, source, preventDefault() }` |
| `onLeave`, `onEnter` | The viewer left a scene, then arrived at one, by any route. | `{ heading, focus, scene, direction }` |
| `onCameraChange` | The camera moved: at most once per drawn frame. | `{ x, y, scale }` |
| `onBackgroundClick` | A confirmed single click or tap on empty space. | `{ screen, world }` |
| `onTextureError` | An item image could not be drawn; once per URL. | `{ url }` |

`direction` is `"in"` (entering an item), `"out"` (zooming out) or `"jump"` (a `focus` change
from outside, such as Back). A scene filling in as it streams, or refreshing after `invalidate`, is
not a navigation and fires neither.

`onNodeActivate` is **cancellable**. By default the explorer enters the item if it can, and for a
pointer otherwise zooms in at that point. Call `preventDefault()` to do something else, such as
opening a panel: tick the box in the demo and double-click a planet.
