When the built-in shapes are not enough, draw an item yourself with PixiJS 8. Give the item a
`kind`, and hand the view a **module of renderers keyed by kind**, loaded lazily. Items whose kind
has no renderer keep their `shape`.

The module it loads. It builds its views from the classes `create(kit)` is handed, and imports
`pixi.js` only as types:

<Code code={RenderersModuleSource.trimEnd()} lang="ts" title="custom-renderers.renderers.ts" />

- **The kit.** `create(kit)` is handed PixiJS's own `Container`, `Graphics`, `Sprite`, `Text` and
  `Texture`: the ones the package draws with. Build from these and import `pixi.js` only with
  `import type`, so PixiJS reaches your page by one lazy path, the package's. A second runtime
  import of `pixi.js` in a lazily loaded module made Bun's bundler emit a PixiJS chunk as one
  app's page entry (it built, then rendered blank). Other bundlers may cope; the kit makes it a
  non-question.
- **The frame** gives `size` (world units: draw centred on 0, 0), `screenSize` (pixels, for level
  of detail), `opacity`, `hovered`, `selected`, `timeMs`, `tint` (the colour the item would have
  had), `texture` (the item's own image, once ready) and `reducedMotion`. The second argument is the
  laid-out item; its `item` field is your item as you wrote it.
- **The explorer still does** culling, transitions, hit-testing, the outline, rings, halos, glows
  and labels. A custom view replaces only the shape.
- **Pooling.** Views are pooled per kind and reused for whichever item comes next, so keep
  per-item state in `update`, not `create`.
- **Animation.** `animated: true` asks for frames while a view of that kind is on screen; under
  `prefers-reduced-motion` none are asked for.
- **Failures.** A kind with no renderer, or a renderer that throws, falls back to the shape and is
  reported once; one that throws is switched off for the session. Throws are isolated; drawing
  mistakes are not, so a broken polygon can still spoil the frame.
- **Cap.** At most `customViews.max` items (200 by default) are drawn by renderers at once; past it,
  those furthest from the middle of the view draw their shape.
- **`@thirkettle/data-explorer/pixi`** holds only the contract's types and `defineRenderers`. Import
  it only from your renderers module, so it and PixiJS stay out of your page's first load.
