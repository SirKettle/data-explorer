The canvas is `aria-hidden`. Beside it, the explorer renders an **outline**: the current scene as
real links (each item's `href`) and buttons (a hollow aggregate), with its badges (`marks`). It is
visually hidden, but read aloud and reachable by keyboard. The demo shows it, so you can see what is
announced as you move.

</Demo>

- **Keyboard.** Tab to an entry and press Enter: the explorer enters that item, raising
  `onNodeActivate` first exactly as a double-click does, then moves focus to the new scene with a
  polite announcement.
- **Every node needs an `href`**, so every relationship drawn on the canvas is also an ordinary
  link. If your data has no pages of its own, use stable in-app URLs.
- **Drawn edges are in the outline too**, as links to their far ends.
- **Without WebGL**, the canvas is skipped and the outline still works.
- **Reduced motion** is respected: no pulse, no grow-in, no `animated` renderers.
